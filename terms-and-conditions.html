1. Description of Service

RozKharcha is a modern, cross-platform personal and household expense management application. Core features include:

  • Personal Expense Tracking: Recording, categorizing, tagging, and organizing personal expenses and transactions.
  • Family & Household Sharing: Shared expense groups allowing authorized family members to pool and monitor household finances together in real time.
  • Offline Guest Mode: Full local-first expense logging with instant undo and offline aggregation without requiring an account.
  • Voice-Assisted Expense Logging: Hands-free expense recording using real-time speech recognition.
  • Receipt Scanning & OCR: Automated parsing of expense details and amounts from receipt images via on-device machine learning.
  • Financial Intelligence & Analytics: Visual category breakdowns, needs vs. wants indicators, and spending runway estimates.
  • Quick Access Extensions: Android home screen App Widgets, app shortcuts, and iOS Siri Shortcuts.
