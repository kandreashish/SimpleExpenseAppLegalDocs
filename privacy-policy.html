Privacy Policy

RozKharcha (Simple Expense Manager)
Android & iOS Offline-First Privacy-Focused
Last Updated: November 2026

Your financial privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy describes how RozKharcha ("we", "our", or "us", operated by Lavish Technologies) collects, handles, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application across Android and iOS platforms.

Summary: We never sell your personal or financial data. You can track expenses entirely offline without an account. When cloud features are enabled, all data transmission and storage adhere to strict industry-standard encryption protocols.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Offline & Guest Mode Data

You can use RozKharcha completely offline in Guest Mode without creating an account or providing any personal information:

  • Local Expense Records: Transaction amounts, dates, categories, and optional descriptions you log are stored exclusively in an encrypted Room/SQLite database on your local device.
  • No Cloud Sync in Guest Mode: Unless you explicitly choose to authenticate and sync, your guest expense records never leave your physical device.

1.2 Authenticated Account Information

When you sign in to sync your expenses across devices or participate in shared family/household expenses, we collect:

Data Type Purpose Storage & Transmission
Google / Apple Identity Secure sign-in, account creation, and user identification Firebase Authentication (OAuth 2.0)
Profile Details (Name, Email, Picture) Personalize your experience and display member identities in shared Family Groups Encrypted Cloud Storage & Local Cache
Expense & Budget Records Personal expense management, budget tracking, and real-time synchronization Encrypted Database (AES-256 local, TLS 1.3 in transit)
Family / Household Group Data Facilitate shared expense pooling among invited household members Encrypted Cloud Backend

1.3 Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

RozKharcha requests only the permissions strictly required to provide its features:

  • Microphone (Voice Expense Input): RozKharcha provides hands-free voice logging. The microphone is accessed only while you actively interact with the voice input button. Audio is converted to text using on-device speech recognition or native platform speech APIs. Audio recordings are never saved, uploaded, or transmitted to our servers or third parties.
  • Camera & Photo Library (Receipt Scanning / OCR): When you choose to scan a physical receipt or pick a receipt image, the app uses on-device optical character recognition (OCR) via Google ML Kit. Receipt images are processed entirely on your device and are never sent to external servers or marketing databases.
  • Notifications: Used only with your explicit permission to deliver helpful budget alerts, family expense updates, or scheduled expense reminders.

1.4 Diagnostics & Usage Analytics

To ensure app reliability, performance, and user satisfaction, we use privacy-conscious analytics tools (Mixpanel and Firebase):

  • Privacy-Preserving Telemetry: In compliance with strict privacy standards, we do not log exact expense amounts, transaction descriptions, voice transcripts, or financial credentials. Telemetry uses aggregate range buckets (e.g., amount buckets, word counts) and pseudonymous device identifiers.
  • Crash Diagnostics: We collect crash reports, error stack traces, and device OS specifications via Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose and resolve software bugs promptly.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Core Expense Management: Calculating daily, weekly, and monthly totals, categorization, and visual financial charts.
  • Household / Family Collaboration: Displaying shared expenses to authenticated members of your chosen family group.
  • Multi-Device Synchronization: Keeping your expense records consistent across multiple phones or tablets.
  • Smart Categorization & Insights: Offering instant category suggestions and spending runway metrics based on your logging habits.
  • Customer Support: Troubleshooting issues and responding to inquiries submitted to our support team.

3. Data Storage, Security, and Protection

3.1 Encryption

Security by Design:

  • Data at Rest: Local authentication tokens and confidential settings are encrypted using Android Keystore and iOS Keychain.
  • Data in Transit: All communication between RozKharcha and cloud servers is strictly encrypted using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Database Integrity: Backend databases employ industry-standard encryption, strict access control, and firewalls.

3.2 Data Isolation

Personal expenses remain completely private to your individual account. In shared Family Groups, only transactions you explicitly mark as Family Expenses are visible to verified members of your family group.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work exclusively with vetted, industry-leading service providers who comply with stringent privacy standards:

  • Google Firebase & Google Cloud: Secure authentication (Google Sign-In), cloud database, push notifications, and crash diagnostics. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Apple Inc.: Apple Sign-In and App Store subscription verification. (Apple Privacy Policy)
  • Mixpanel: Privacy-first product usage analytics using anonymized metrics without financial details. (Mixpanel Privacy Policy)

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We will never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or financial data to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

Information is shared only under these narrow conditions:

  • Within Your Family Group: With family members you have explicitly authorized by creating or joining a household.
  • Trusted Infrastructure Providers: Strictly as required to operate the cloud infrastructure (Firebase/cloud host) under confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Obligations: If mandated by law, court order, or official regulatory process.

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Complete Data Control

  • Access & Export: You can view all your logged transactions anytime within the app.
  • Correction: You have full control to edit or update any transaction, budget, or profile detail directly.
  • Instant Undo & Deletion: Delete single transactions, clear local guest databases, or remove family records at your discretion.

6.2 Account & Data Deletion

You have the absolute right to permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time:

  1. Open RozKharcha and navigate to Profile / Settings.
  2. Select Account Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm your request. All associated user profile records, authentication credentials, and cloud-synced personal expense records will be permanently purged within 30 days.

Alternatively, you may request account and data deletion by emailing our support team at kashishcool144@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us and we will promptly remove such information.
